Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the app service provider (the "Operator") handles personal information in operating the MartGaja app (Android).
Effective date: May 21, 2026

1. Personal information the Operator directly collects

MartGaja has no sign-up, login, or social login.
The shopping list and category data you enter are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to or stored on the Operator's servers.

✅ The Operator does not directly collect any of your personal information.

2. Information collected by a third party (Google) — Advertising

This app serves banner ads through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, Google may automatically collect the following information.

Data collectedCollected byPurpose
Advertising ID (GAID)Google AdMobPersonalized advertising
Device info (OS version, device name)Google AdMobAd optimization
IP addressGoogle AdMobRegion-based advertising
App usage patternsGoogle AdMobMeasuring ad display frequency

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
To reset the advertising ID (GAID) or disable ad personalization: available on your device under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

3. Information collected by a third party (Google) — App analytics (Firebase Analytics)

This app uses Google Firebase Analytics to improve the service. Firebase Analytics anonymously collects and analyzes app usage patterns, and the collected information is used only in aggregated, statistical form.

Data collectedCollected byPurpose
App instance ID (anonymous)Google FirebaseDistinguishing devices (no personal identification)
Device info (OS version, device model)Google FirebaseDevice compatibility analysis
Country / language infoGoogle FirebaseRegional usage statistics
App open/close, screen transition eventsGoogle FirebaseUsage pattern analysis and service improvement
Feature usage events (adding items, selecting categories, etc.)Google FirebasePrioritizing improvements to key features

The collected data is processed only as anonymous statistics that cannot identify individuals, and is not sold to third parties.
Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
To disable data collection: on your device under Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization, or by turning off "Usage & diagnostics" sharing in device settings.

4. Information collected by a third party (Google Play) — In-app purchases

If you purchase the "Remove Ads" feature, it is processed through the Google Play billing system. Payment information (card number, transaction history, etc.) is managed by Google, and the Operator only receives whether the payment succeeded. The Operator does not store or access payment information.

5. Internet access

The app connects to the internet to load ads, process purchases, and send app usage statistics. All core features—such as adding and checking off shopping list items—work fully offline, even without internet. Analytics events generated while offline are temporarily stored on the device and sent automatically when a network connection is available.

6. Children's privacy

MartGaja is not directed at children under the age of 14. Users under 14 should use the app with the consent of a guardian.

7. Changes to this policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, we will give notice through this page 7 days before the change takes effect. Significant changes will be separately announced via a pop-up when the app is updated.

8. Contact

For inquiries regarding personal information, please contact us at:
